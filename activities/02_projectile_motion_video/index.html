Key Concepts

📐 Trajectory

Projectile motion follows a parabolic path due to constant horizontal velocity and constant downward acceleration (gravity).

y = x·tan(θ) - (g·x²)/(2·v₀²·cos²(θ))

➡️ Horizontal Motion

Horizontal velocity remains constant (no air resistance). Distance = velocity × time.

x = v₀·cos(θ)·t

⬇️ Vertical Motion

Vertical motion is affected by gravity. Velocity decreases going up, increases going down.

y = v₀·sin(θ)·t - ½·g·t²

🎯 Range

Maximum range occurs at 45° launch angle (in vacuum). Range depends on initial velocity and angle.

R = (v₀²·sin(2θ))/g
